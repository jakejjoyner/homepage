AILedger (alpha)

Open-source AI inference logging infrastructure for EU AI Act Article 12 compliance.

A drop-in Cloudflare Workers proxy that sits between an AI app and its provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini). Every inference becomes a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit record: any later change is detectable. Raw prompts and outputs are never stored — only SHA-256 fingerprints — so it's GDPR-clean by design. Tamper-evident chain shipped to prod 2026-04 (verify_chain SQL function lets any auditor independently reproduce the chain with standard SHA-256). Durable-buffer audit-write hardening shipped 2026-05: audit records are durably committed to a Cloudflare KV write-buffer before the response returns, with a scheduled drain to Postgres — surviving database outage and worker crash mid-flight. Compliance report exports to PDF in one click.

Cloudflare Workers, Supabase, TypeScript, Postgres
